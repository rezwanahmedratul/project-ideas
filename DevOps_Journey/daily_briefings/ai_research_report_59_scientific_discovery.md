## Overview

AI's application to scientific discovery has moved beyond辅助工具 to become an independent research methodology. By August 2026, AI systems are not just analyzing existing data — they're generating hypotheses, designing experiments, and discovering new scientific phenomena.

## Key Breakthroughs

### Drug Discovery
- Generative models now design novel molecular structures with desired properties
- AlphaFold-era breakthroughs extended to protein-ligand binding prediction
- AI-accelerated clinical trial simulation reducing development timelines
- Multiple AI-discovered compounds entering Phase I trials in 2026

### Climate and Environmental Science
- AI models predicting local climate impacts with unprecedented resolution
- Ocean current and weather pattern forecasting improved by 20-30%
- Carbon capture material discovery accelerated through generative chemistry
- Satellite imagery analysis for deforestation and pollution detection

### Physics and Simulation
- Neural operators replacing traditional numerical methods for PDEs
- 1000x speedup in certain simulation tasks while maintaining accuracy
- Discovery of new material properties through inverse design
- Fusion energy plasma control via reinforcement learning

### Mathematics
- AI systems contributing to open mathematical problems
- Proof assistance and conjecture generation
- Formal verification of complex mathematical arguments

---

## The Research Pipeline

```
Traditional: Hypothesis → Experiment → Analysis → Theory
AI-Augmented: Data → Pattern Discovery → Hypothesis Generation → 
              Experiment Design → Validation → New Theory
```

AI excels at the pattern discovery and hypothesis generation phases, while humans remain essential for experimental design and interpretation.

---

## Why It Matters

1. **Acceleration** — Scientific discovery timelines compressed from years to months
2. **New paradigms** — AI discovers patterns invisible to human analysts
3. **Interdisciplinary** — AI bridges domains (biology + chemistry + computation)
4. **Accessibility** — Researchers without massive computational resources can participate
5. **Homelab angle** — Lightweight AI models can assist with personal research projects
