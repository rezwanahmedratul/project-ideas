## Current Limitations

1. **Qubit Count**: Useful applications require 1000+ error-corrected qubits; current leaders have ~1000 physical qubits
2. **Noise Sensitivity**: Decoherence limits circuit depth for complex molecules
3. **Algorithm Maturity**: Many QML algorithms lack rigorous complexity proofs for practical regimes
4. **Domain Expertise Gap**: Chemistry/physics experts must learn quantum computing fundamentals
