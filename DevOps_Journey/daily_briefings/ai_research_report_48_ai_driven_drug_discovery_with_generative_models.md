# AI-Driven Drug Discovery with Generative Models

## Overview
The intersection of AI and pharmaceutical research has entered a new era in 2026. Generative models can now design novel molecular structures with desired therapeutic properties, predict binding affinity, and simulate clinical outcomes — compressing drug discovery timelines from years to months.

## Key Breakthroughs
- **Generative molecular design**: Models like AlphaFold 3 successors and RFdiffusion generate entirely new molecular scaffolds optimized for target protein binding, moving beyond screening existing compound libraries.
- **Toxicity and side-effect prediction**: Multi-modal models predict ADMET (absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion, toxicity) properties from molecular structure alone, filtering out dangerous candidates before synthesis.
- **Clinical trial simulation**: AI creates synthetic control arms and predicts patient response distributions, reducing the number of real-world trial participants needed.
- **Repurposing at scale**: Graph neural networks map drug-target-disease relationships across millions of compounds, identifying off-label uses for existing medications in days rather than years.
