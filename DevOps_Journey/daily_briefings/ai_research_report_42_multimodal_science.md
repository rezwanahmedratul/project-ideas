## Overview

Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) are emerging as powerful tools for scientific discovery in 2026. By combining vision, language, and reasoning capabilities, these models can analyze experimental data, interpret scientific imagery, and generate hypotheses across disciplines.

## What Are Large Multimodal Models?

LMMs extend large language models with:
- **Vision encoders** — Process images, diagrams, micrographs
- **Scientific domain knowledge** — Trained on research papers, textbooks, datasets
- **Reasoning capabilities** — Chain-of-thought for complex scientific problems
- **Tool use** — Access to simulations, databases, calculation engines

## Challenge: Hallucination in Science

Scientific applications demand extreme accuracy. LMMs can:
- Confidently state incorrect facts
- Misinterpret diagrams
- Generate plausible but wrong equations
- Overlook critical details in complex images

**Mitigation strategies:**
1. **Tool-augmented reasoning** — Verify claims with calculation engines
2. **Uncertainty quantification** — Model outputs confidence scores
3. **Human-in-the-loop** — Critical decisions require expert validation
4. **Citation grounding** — Require sources for factual claims
