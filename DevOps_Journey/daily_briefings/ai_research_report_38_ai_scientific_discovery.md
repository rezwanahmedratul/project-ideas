## Challenges & Critiques

1. **Overconfidence** — Tools rate their own accuracy higher than warranted
2. **Physical impossibility** — Generated structures may violate chemical rules
3. **Reproducibility** — Black-box models make validation difficult
4. **Bias in training data** — Underrepresentation of non-European biological samples
