## Benchmark Saturation Concerns

1. **Data Contamination**: As models are trained on more internet data, benchmark questions may appear in training sets
2. **Overfitting to Benchmarks**: Specialized tuning for specific evaluations doesn't generalize
3. **Borrowed Intelligence**: Models may be using search/retrieval during evaluation
4. **Human Grade Ceiling**: Some benchmarks now exceed average human performance, raising questions about usefulness

## New Evaluation Paradigms

### Live Competition Format
- Real-time problem solving with time pressure
- Multiple attempts allowed
- Peer comparison

### Dynamic Benchmark Generation
- Continuously generated problems
- Adaptive difficulty based on performance
- Eliminates memorization concerns

### Multi-Agent Collaboration Tests
- Evaluate teams of AI agents working together
- Measure coordination, delegation, communication
