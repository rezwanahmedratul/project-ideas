- **Deductive**: Logical inference from theories

## Challenges and Limitations

### Epistemological
- **Correlation vs causation**: AI finds patterns, not necessarily causes
- **Theory-laden observation**: Background assumptions affect data interpretation
- **Replicability crisis**: Computational reproducibility challenges

### Practical
- **Data quality**: Garbage in, garbage out
- **Computational resources**: Expensive simulations
- **Expert validation**: Human oversight still essential

### Ethical
- **Authorship**: Who gets credit for AI-assisted discoveries?
- **Bias amplification**: Historical biases in training data
- **Safety**: Autonomous experimentation risks

## The Human-AI Collaboration Model

### Most Effective Patterns
1. **AI suggests, human decides**: Recommendation systems with human oversight
2. **Human-guided AI exploration**: Researchers steer AI toward interesting areas
3. **Iterative refinement**: Back-and-forth between AI and human experts
4. **Division of labor**: AI handles computation, humans handle insight

### Success Metrics
- Number of testable hypotheses generated
- Fraction of hypotheses experimentally verified
- Time from hypothesis to validation
- Novelty and impact of resulting publications
