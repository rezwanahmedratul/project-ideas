## Overview

The scientific method relies on hypothesis generation as a creative, insight-driven process. AI systems are now demonstrating capability to propose novel, testable hypotheses by identifying patterns, contradictions, and opportunities in vast scientific literature and experimental data. This report examines recent advances in AI-driven hypothesis generation across disciplines.

## Methodology

### Literature Mining Approach

1. **Text extraction**: Pulling findings from millions of papers
2. **Relation extraction**: Building knowledge graphs of scientific claims
3. **Gap identification**: Finding unexplained phenomena or contradictory results
4. **Hypothesis synthesis**: Combining insights to propose new explanations

### Data Integration Approach

1. **Multi-omics integration**: Combining genomics, proteomics, metabolomics data
2. **Experimental condition mapping**: Linking results across studies
3. **Phenotype-genotype correlation**: Finding connections between observations
4. **Causal inference**: Distinguishing correlation from causation
