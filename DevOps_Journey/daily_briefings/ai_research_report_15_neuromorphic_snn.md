## Key Research Areas

### 1. Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)

SNNs process information through discrete spikes rather than continuous activations:
- **Energy efficiency**: 100-1000x lower power than traditional ANNs
- **Temporal processing**: Native handling of time-series data
- **Event-driven computation**: Only active neurons consume power

**Breakthroughs (2025-2026):**
- SNNs achieving competitive accuracy on image classification (MNIST, CIFAR-10)
- Hybrid SNN-ANN approaches for improved performance
- Training algorithms: temporal backpropagation, surrogate gradients

### 3. Theoretical Advances

- **Liquid neural networks**: Continuous-time RNNs with neurodynamic constraints
- **Memory-augmented SNNs**: Combining working memory with spiking dynamics
- **Neuromodulation**: Dopamine-like signaling for reinforcement in SNNs
