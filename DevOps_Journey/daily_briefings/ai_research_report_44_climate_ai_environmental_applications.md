# Climate AI and Environmental Applications

## Overview
Artificial intelligence is being deployed at scale to address climate change, from carbon emissions tracking to biodiversity monitoring and renewable energy optimization. The 2025-2026 period saw a dramatic increase in climate-focused AI applications funded by both public and private sector initiatives.

## Key Breakthroughs
- **Satellite-based emissions monitoring**: AI models analyze satellite imagery to detect methane leaks, deforestation, and industrial emissions in near real-time (e.g., Google's MethaneSAT AI pipeline).
- **Climate modeling acceleration**: Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) speed up climate simulations by 100-1000x while maintaining accuracy, enabling higher-resolution forecasts.
- **Smart grid optimization**: Reinforcement learning agents balance renewable energy supply and demand across electrical grids, reducing curtailment and improving stability.
- **Biodiversity tracking**: Acoustic AI sensors monitor forest and ocean ecosystems, identifying species populations from audio and visual data at continental scales.
