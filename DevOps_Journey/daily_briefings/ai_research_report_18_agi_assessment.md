## Overview

As AI capabilities approach human-level performance on narrow tasks, the question of **Artificial General Intelligence** becomes empirically tractable. This report covers new evaluation frameworks designed to measure breadth, adaptability, and reasoning — key dimensions of general intelligence.

---

## AGI Dimensions Being Measured

### 1. Breadth of Knowledge
- Multidisciplinary competency
- Cross-domain transfer
- Zero-shot adaptability

### 2. Reasoning Complexity
- Multi-step logical deduction
- Abstract pattern recognition
- Strategic planning under uncertainty

### 3. Social & Emotional Intelligence
- Theory of mind assessment
- Empathetic response generation
- Collaborative problem-solving

### 4. Creativity & Novelty
- Original idea generation
- Analogical reasoning
- Humor and irony comprehension

## Key Debate: Narrow vs. General

The field is split on:
- Whether current capabilities represent **narrow superhuman** or **emerging general** intelligence
- How to define operational thresholds for AGI
- Whether benchmark gaming inflates measured capability
