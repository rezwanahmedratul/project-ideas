## Executive Summary

Causal AI combines causal inference with deep learning to provide explainable, robust decisions. Moving beyond correlation-based patterns, causal models enable counterfactual reasoning and mechanistic understanding—critical for high-stakes domains like healthcare and finance.

---

## Key Frameworks

### 1. Neural Causal Models (NCM)
Combines neural networks with structural causal models for:
- End-to-end causal representation learning
- Do-calculus operations on neural architectures
- Interpretability with predictive power

### 2. Causal Transformer
Transformer architectures with causal attention mechanisms:
- Explicit dependency graphs
- Interventional reasoning capabilities
- Robustness to distribution shift

### 3. Structural Equation Modeling + DL
Hybrid approaches integrating domain knowledge:
- Physics-informed neural operators
- Causal graph discovery from data
- Policy learning with causal constraints
