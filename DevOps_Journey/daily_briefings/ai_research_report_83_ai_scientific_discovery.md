## The AI Scientist Paradigm

Perhaps the most significant trend in 2026 is the emergence of **autonomous AI scientists** — systems that can conduct entire research cycles autonomously:

1. **Literature review** — AI surveys existing research and identifies knowledge gaps
2. **Hypothesis generation** — AI proposes testable hypotheses
3. **Experiment design** — AI designs experiments (virtual or physical)
4. **Data collection** — AI controls robotic labs or runs simulations
5. **Analysis** — AI analyzes results and iterates
