# AI-Driven Drug Discovery and Computational Biology

## Overview
AI is revolutionizing drug discovery and biological research by accelerating target identification, molecular generation, and clinical trial design. Models like AlphaFold 3, RoseTTAFold All-Atom, and proprietary platforms from Recursion Pharmaceuticals and Insilico Medicine are compressing years of research into months.

## Key Breakthroughs
- **Protein structure prediction**: AlphaFold 3 (2025) achieved near-experimental accuracy in predicting protein-ligand and protein-DNA interactions, enabling rational drug design at scale.
- **Generative molecular design**: Diffusion models and reinforcement learning agents generate novel molecular structures optimized for binding affinity, solubility, and low toxicity.
- **Virtual clinical trials**: AI simulations model patient population responses to drugs, reducing the number of required Phase II/III participants.
- **Biomarker discovery**: ML models scan genomic, proteomic, and metabolomic datasets to identify novel therapeutic targets.
