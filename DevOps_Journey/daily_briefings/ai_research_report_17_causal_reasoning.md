## Overview

Current deep learning excels at pattern recognition but struggles with **causal reasoning**. The 2025-2026 research frontier focuses on embedding causal models into AI systems to improve generalization, interpretability, and robustness.

---

## Core Concepts

### Pearl's Ladder of Causation

| Level | Question | Capability |
|-------|----------|------------|
| 1 — Association | What if I see X? | Pattern matching |
| 2 — Intervention | What if I do X? | Active learning, RL |
| 3 — Counterfactual | What if I had done X? | Explanation, regret |

Most current LLMs operate at Level 1. New research pushes toward Levels 2 and 3.

### Structural Causal Models (SCMs)

- **Graphical models** representing causal relationships
- **Do-calculus** for reasoning about interventions
- **Backdoor/frontdoor criteria** for confounding adjustment

---

## Recent Breakthroughs

### 1. Causal Representation Learning

- Discovering latent causal variables from observations
- Separating invariant mechanisms from spurious correlations
- Applications in robust ML and OOD generalization

### 2. Causal LLMs

- **Causal prompting**: Structured queries that isolate causal effects
- **Interventional fine-tuning**: Training on do(X=x) scenarios
- **Counterfactual generation**: "What would have happened if..."

### 3. Causal RL

- Decision-making with explicit causal structure
- Transfer learning across environments with shared causality
- Sample-efficient exploration using causal graphs
