## Overview

Edge AI has evolved from simple inference on microcontrollers to complex reasoning systems running entirely on-device. Modern smartphones, laptops, and IoT devices now host models capable of natural language understanding, computer vision, and even basic agentic reasoning — all without cloud connectivity. This eliminates latency, preserves privacy, and reduces infrastructure costs.
