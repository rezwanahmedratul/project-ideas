## Executive Summary

Quantum computing is poised to revolutionize drug discovery by simulating molecular interactions at unprecedented scales. While still in early stages, hybrid quantum-classical approaches are already identifying promising drug candidates that classical computers cannot efficiently model.

---

## Key Developments

### 1. Quantum Molecular Simulation
Quantum computers can naturally represent quantum systems, enabling accurate simulation of:
- Protein-ligand binding energies
- Electronic structure calculations
- Reaction pathway optimization

### 2. Hybrid Quantum-Classical Algorithms
Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE) and Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) enable practical drug discovery on near-term quantum hardware.

### 3. Error-Corrected Computing
New fault-tolerant architectures promise to unlock full quantum advantage for pharmaceutical applications by 2027-2028.
