# AI in Cryptography and Security Research

## Overview
The intersection of AI and cryptography represents one of the most consequential frontiers in modern security research. In 2026, AI is being used both to break existing cryptographic schemes and to design provably secure protocols — creating an ongoing arms race between offensive and defensive AI security research.

## Key Breakthroughs
- **Side-channel attack automation**: ML models analyze power consumption and timing data from hardware implementations to extract cryptographic keys without physical access.
- **Post-quantum cryptography validation**: AI tools verify the security of NIST-standardized post-quantum algorithms (Kyber, Dilithium, Falcon) against known and emerging attack vectors.
- **Formal verification of smart contracts**: AI-assisted theorem provers automatically detect reentrancy, overflow, and logic vulnerabilities in DeFi protocols.
- **Adversarial robustness auditing**: Automated red-teaming pipelines probe AI models for jailbreak vulnerabilities, prompt injection flaws, and data leakage.
