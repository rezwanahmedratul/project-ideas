## Overview

While large language models dominate headlines, the field is experiencing a quiet revolution in small language models (SLMs) — models under 10 billion parameters that offer comparable performance on many tasks while being more efficient to train and deploy. Efficient training methodologies are also advancing rapidly, making high-quality AI more accessible to smaller organizations.
