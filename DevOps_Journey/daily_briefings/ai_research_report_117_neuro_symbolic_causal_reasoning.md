## Overview

Neuro-symbolic AI represents a convergence of neural networks (subsymbolic, pattern-based reasoning) and symbolic AI (logic-based, rule-based reasoning). This hybrid approach addresses fundamental limitations of pure neural approaches, particularly in causal reasoning, interpretability, and sample efficiency.

## The Neuro-Symbolic Convergence

### Why Combine Approaches?

Pure neural networks face inherent limitations:
- **Black-box reasoning**: Difficulty understanding decision pathways
- **Poor generalization**: Struggle with out-of-distribution scenarios
- **Data hunger**: Require massive training datasets
- **Causal blind spots**: Correlation ≠ causation

Symbolic AI offers complementary strengths:
- **Explicit reasoning**: Transparent logical deduction
- **Knowledge representation**: Structured domain knowledge
- **Causal modeling**: Explicit cause-effect relationships
- **Sample efficiency**: Learn from fewer examples

## Key Breakthroughs (2025-2026)

### 1. Differentiable Logic Layers

Research has produced neural architectures that embed logical operations:
- **Neural Theorem Provers**: End-to-end trainable proof systems
- **Logic tensor networks**: Combining first-order logic with embeddings
- **Relational neural manipulation**: Differentiable symbolic operations

### 2. Causal Discovery from Data

Advances in automated causal reasoning:
- **Neural causal models**: Learning causal graphs from observations
- **Interventional reasoning**: Simulating "what-if" scenarios
- **Counterfactual generation**: Understanding alternative outcomes

### 3. Program Synthesis with Neural Guidance

Neural networks guiding symbolic program construction:
- **Synthval**: Generating programs that satisfy specifications
- **Neural guided search**: Efficient exploration of program space
- **Verification-aware synthesis**: Ensuring correctness guarantees
