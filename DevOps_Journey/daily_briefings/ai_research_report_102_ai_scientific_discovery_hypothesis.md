## Challenges and Limitations

### Hallucination in Science
- Generated references may not exist
- Fake citations in AI literature reviews
- Need for rigorous verification

### Interpretability
- Black-box models lack mechanistic understanding
- Scientists need causal explanations
- Must bridge correlation and causation

### Reproducibility
- AI models may not reproduce results
- Randomness in generation process
- Importance of version control

### Bias in Training Data
- Historical biases in scientific literature
- Underrepresentation of certain regions
- Western-centric knowledge dominance
