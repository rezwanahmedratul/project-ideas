# AI-Driven Scientific Simulation at Scale

## Overview
Scientific simulation — from fluid dynamics to quantum chemistry to astrophysics — has historically required supercomputer-grade resources. In 2026, AI surrogate models trained on high-fidelity simulations enable near-instantaneous predictions, opening new possibilities for iterative research and real-time decision-making.

## Key Breakthroughs
- **Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs)**: Networks constrained by conservation laws (mass, energy, momentum) learn to solve PDEs orders of magnitude faster than traditional finite-element methods.
- **Quantum chemistry accelerators**: AI models predict molecular energies and reaction pathways with chemical accuracy, replacing DFT calculations that previously took hours per molecule.
- **Weather forecasting at hyperresolution**: Graph neural networks now produce 10-day global forecasts at 1km resolution in minutes — matching or exceeding numerical weather prediction models.
- **Digital twins for engineering**: AI-driven simulation twins of bridges, aircraft, and power plants provide real-time structural health monitoring and predictive maintenance.
